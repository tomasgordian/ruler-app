Ruler
Android screen ruler

Measure anything on your screen.

A ruler that floats over any app, so you can check margins, padding, icon sizes and alignment right where they are. Read it in dp, px or sp, and zoom in to place each edge on an exact pixel. Made for developers, testers and designers.

Get it onGoogle Play Free, no ads, no account. Captured frames never leave your device.
  • 01

    Width or height

    Measure across with the horizontal ruler or down with the vertical one, and switch with one tap. It works in portrait and landscape, and guidelines run across the screen to line up distant edges.

  • 02

    A magnifier that sees under the ruler

    Zoom in on the screen beneath the edge you're moving until you see individual pixels. Nudge the edge one unit at a time and watch exactly where it lands. Switch edges with one tap.

  • 03

    dp, px and sp

    sp follows your font-size setting, so text measures the way Android draws it. Snap to the 4 dp grid to check spacing.

  • 04

    Type an exact size

    Enter a value, like 375 dp, and the ruler resizes to match.

  • 05

    Any colour you like

    Red, green, blue, or any colour you pick, so the ruler stands out on light and dark apps.

  • 06

    Works over any app

    The ruler floats on top and the app underneath stays usable. Tuck it into a bubble at the screen edge, or drag it onto × to close. Add the Quick Settings tile to turn it on from any app without opening Ruler.
